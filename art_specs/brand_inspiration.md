# DiNapoli's Brand Inspiration — Intake Notes (2026-08-10)

One AI-generated inspiration board is on file, stored privately as
`.private_art/brand/brand_board_a_plaque.png`: the DINAPOLI'S plaque
lockup (cream slab lettering on an Oxblood field, gold inner rule) with
the yellow-ring "D" monogram carrying pepperoni dots, plus a navy
monogram variant. It is 1536×1024 high-resolution flat illustration,
not pixel art. Per `color_language.md` §12, storefront/title identity
exploration begins only after the four scene studies — these notes are
intake and reconciliation, not identity work.

A second board (a "PIZZA"-subtitle variant whose monogram circle was
drawn as a pizza pan with handles) was reviewed and **rejected by the
user as a bad fit for PixelLab**; it has been deleted and is not part
of the inspiration set. Board A is the sole brand reference.

Conclusion: the brand board independently lands on the color language's
DiNapoli's identity (`#680828` shadow, `#A81031` sauce, cheese-gold
lettering, Flour highlight) — no palette conflict, no new tokens
needed. When the signage experiment (asset queue item 4) eventually
runs, the brand translation is a quantization of this board onto
existing native tokens, with the navy choice and the gold step
(`#FFE976` vs `#DFBB02`) as the two recorded decisions to make.
