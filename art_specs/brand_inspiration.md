# DiNapoli's Brand Inspiration — Intake Notes (2026-08-10)

Two AI-generated inspiration boards were provided by the user and are
stored privately as `.private_art/brand/brand_board_a_plaque.png`
(plaque lockup, yellow-ring "D" monogram with pepperoni dots, navy
monogram variant) and `.private_art/brand/brand_board_b_pizza_subtitle.png`
(adds a gold "PIZZA" subtitle; the monogram circle is drawn as a pizza
pan with handles). Both are 1536×1024 high-resolution flat illustration,
not pixel art. Per `color_language.md` §12, storefront/title identity
exploration begins only after the four scene studies — these notes are
intake and reconciliation, not identity work.

## Sampled swatches vs. the color language

Swatch strips sampled programmatically (dominant color of a 40×40 patch
per swatch; board A / board B):

| Brand swatch | Sampled | Nearest native token | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burgundy | `#6a0b20` / `#5e1119` | Oxblood `#680828` | Near-exact; the brand's plaque field IS Oxblood |
| Red | `#aa1627` / `#ac2b2e` | DiNapoli's sauce red `#A81031` | Near-exact; confirms deep sauce red as brand, not Critical `#FF2B3C` |
| Navy | `#213250` / `#1c314d` | Between Old Harbor `#152653` and Carbon Ink `#303B5A` | Both natives available; pick per surface (sign shadow vs. ink) |
| Cream | `#f6e2bd` / `#f6e5c3` | Between Flour `#FBFBE8` and Oven Paper `#F8D088` | Quantizes cleanly to the existing warm-paper ramp |
| Gold | `#fecc54` / `#f9c86b` | Cheese peak `#FFE976` (then `#DFBB02`) | Supports the deferred `#FFE976` as the eventual lettering/ring gold |
| Background | `#fcf3e2` | Flour `#FBFBE8` | Consistent |

Conclusion: the brand boards independently land on the color language's
DiNapoli's identity (`#680828` shadow, `#A81031` sauce, cheese-gold
lettering, Flour highlight) — no palette conflict, no new tokens
needed. When the signage experiment (asset queue item 4) eventually
runs, the brand translation is a quantization of these boards onto
existing native tokens, with the navy choice and the gold step
(`#FFE976` vs `#DFBB02`) as the two recorded decisions to make.
